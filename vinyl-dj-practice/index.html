How to practice
  1. Load a track on Deck A, hit Play.
  2. Load a second track on Deck B. Hold CUE to preview in your headphones (split-cue).
  3. Drag the pitch fader on Deck B until the tempo sounds close.
  4. Tap Nudge + / Nudge − to push the beat into phase.
  5. Release CUE on a downbeat, slide the crossfader, and keep nudging to hold phase.
  6. Switch to Vinyl mode to hide BPM numbers and mix purely by ear.
